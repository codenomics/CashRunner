## Getting started

```
CASHRUNNER
==========

A weekly budget. Add your paycheck, bills, credit cards/loans and savings,
and CashRunner breaks everything down into weekly amounts and shows what's
SPENDABLE each week after bills.


GETTING STARTED
---------------
Pick one. Both give you the same app.

OPTION 1 - INSTALLER (recommended)
  Download the file ending in _Setup.exe, double-click it and click Install.
  It installs just for you (no admin password) and adds Start menu and
  Desktop shortcuts. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).
  To remove it later: Windows Settings > Apps > CashRunner > Uninstall.

OPTION 2 - NO INSTALL (zip)
  1. Download the file ending in _no-install.zip. Right-click it -> Extract
  All... and put the CashRunner folder somewhere it can stay (for example
  Documents). Don't run it from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click CashRunner.exe. Nothing is installed; to remove it, delete
  the folder.

EITHER WAY
  Optional: pin it to Start (right-click it in the Start menu or on the
  desktop -> Pin to Start).

"Windows protected your PC"? Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
Windows shows that for apps downloaded from the internet that aren't
signed with a paid certificate.


USING IT
--------
1. Under INCOME click "+ Add income" and add your paycheck:
   how often you're paid, which day, and the amount of each check.
   "Changes each check" = you type in each check on payday instead.
2. Add your bills, credit cards/loans and savings the same way.
   Each one shows what it comes to per week.
3. The three boxes at the top show your weekly income, weekly bills and
   what's SPENDABLE each week.

- Tick the round box on a bill when you've paid it. It clears itself when
  the next one comes due.
- AUTOPAY: set it to "Yes, it pays itself" on bills that come out of your
  account by themselves. They're marked paid on each due date, show an
  AUTOPAY tag, and don't send reminders.
- Credit cards/loans: ticking a payment takes it off the balance. When the
  balance hits zero it moves to "Paid off" and Spendable goes up.
  Update the balance from your statement now and then (click the card).
- Savings: ticking adds the amount to "saved so far". When you reach the
  goal it moves to "Goals reached".
- UPCOMING on the right lists what's due in the next 7 or 14 days, plus
  paydays.
- PAY CALCULATOR (top right): type your hourly wage and the hours you
  worked to see the check - time and a half over 40 hours a week, for a
  1- or 2-week pay period. Add a take-home % (what reaches your bank out
  of your gross pay) for a closer number. If you have an income set to
  "Changes each check", it can put the result in as that check.
- Click anything to change it. Right-click for more (like taking back a
  payment you ticked by mistake).


REMINDERS
---------
When you close the window, CashRunner keeps running in the taskbar corner
(next to the clock) and pops up a reminder when bills are due.
Settings > REMIND ME picks when (on the day, a day before, 3 days before).
Settings > START WITH WINDOWS starts it quietly in the taskbar corner.
To really quit: right-click the icon in the taskbar corner -> Quit.


PRIVACY AND GOOGLE DRIVE
------------------------
- Your budget is saved encrypted, locked to your Windows account on this
  PC. Copied anywhere else, it's unreadable.
- Settings > GOOGLE DRIVE SYNC > Turn on puts a copy in your Google Drive
  (through the Google Drive for desktop app), locked with a password you
  pick. On another PC, run CashRunner, turn on sync and enter the same
  password. The password can't be recovered if you forget it.
- Settings > LOCKED COPY saves or opens a password-locked copy anywhere.
- Settings > BACKUPS: a copy is kept each day (last 30 days).


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Your budget, settings and backups are in %APPDATA%\CashRunner.
- If something goes wrong, CashRunner-log.txt next to CashRunner.exe says
  what (it never contains names or amounts).
- To remove CashRunner: right-click its icon in the taskbar corner -> Quit,
  turn off Settings > START WITH WINDOWS first if it's on, then delete its
  folder, plus %APPDATA%\CashRunner (that deletes your budget too).
```
